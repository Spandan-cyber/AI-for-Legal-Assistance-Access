Platform Features

Everything you need to
understand your rights.

Six powerful AI-driven tools that work together to give you full clarity over any legal document.

Plain-Language Simplifier
Every dense, jargon-heavy clause translated side-by-side into plain English you can actually understand. No legal degree required. Includes "Watch Out" trap callouts for hidden gotchas.
Contract Comparison Diff
Upload two versions of a contract and instantly see what changed — salary cuts, expanded non-competes, removed severance — highlighted clause by clause.
Risk & Clause Radar
Every clause automatically rated High / Medium / Low risk and categorized by type: Payment, IP Ownership, Termination, Non-Compete, Liability, and more.
Interactive Document Q&A
Chat directly with your document. Ask "Who owns my side projects?" or "Can I be fired without cause?" and get contextual answers with exact clause citations.
Lawyer Consultation Prep Kit
Auto-generates a complete attorney briefing document with: an executive summary, top 5 red flags to negotiate, and a prioritized list of questions to ask in your 30-minute consultation. Exportable as Markdown instantly.
